In companies you have applied a lot. \
But a romance internship you forgot? \
 \
No, the high school love doesn’t count, \
Carefree idiots, forgive me, I discount. \
 \
The toxic first love when you are twenty \
Here insecurity and jealousy are plenty. \
 \
Where you are ready to let go of a dream, \
When a two-year relationship is supreme. \
 \
A tit-for-tat system works like a charm. \
Breaks from each other do only harm. \
 \
Either on a pedestal or a ditch, \
You have put the asshole/bitch. \
 \
Letting go of hobbies one-by-one, \
Neglecting yourself you begin. \
 \
The ever evident promise of change \
On the horizon never looks strange. \
 \
Unable to express stress past her, \
Avoiding conflicts you will master. \
 \
Ample room for learning the internship offers, \
Leaving will surely fill your coffers. \
